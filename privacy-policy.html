Streak Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Streak ("the app," "we," "us") is a habit-tracking app. This policy explains what information the app uses, how it's stored, and the choices you have. We've written it to match exactly what the app currently does — we'll update it if that changes.

Information You Provide

When you create a habit in Streak, you enter a habit name and choose an icon. This information is used only to display your habits and streaks within the app.

Habit and Streak Data

Your habits, streak counts, and daily completion history are stored on your device so the app can show your progress and calendar history. We do not currently sell, rent, or share this data with third parties for advertising purposes.

Account Sign-In

Streak may offer the option to sign in with a third-party account (such as Google) to keep your data linked to your account. If you use this option, we receive basic profile information (such as your name and email address) from that provider in order to create and identify your account. We do not post on your behalf or access other data from that account.

Subscriptions and Payment

Streak offers an optional paid subscription for unlimited habits. Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Apple handles all billing in accordance with their own privacy policy.
